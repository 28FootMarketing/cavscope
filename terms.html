2. What CavScope is

CavScope is a website assurance service. It assesses a website you register from the outside, the way an anonymous visitor or a crawler meets it: HTTPS and response headers, cookies, redirects, public DNS and email-authentication records, exposed files, privacy and legal signals on the page, accessibility, and AI readiness. It keeps the results in a risk register and a control register, scores a posture, and writes a plain-English report called a SITREP.

The scanner identifies itself as CavScope-Scanner/1.0. It does not sign in, does not submit forms, and does not try to reach anything a visitor could not reach. It renders no JavaScript unless the browser engine has been enabled for your organization, and it never attempts to exploit a weakness it reports.

6. What a SITREP is, and what it is not

A SITREP is a technical assessment of what the engine observed on a date. It is not a compliance certification, an audit opinion, a legal opinion, or legal advice. In particular:

  • A framework reference on a finding is a citation that says where a finding belongs. It never says CavScope tests the whole framework. We do not provide a SOC 2 opinion; that is a licensed CPA firm's examination.
  • A clean result is evidence that the listed checks passed on that date. It is not a statement that the site is secure, accessible or compliant.
  • The laws listed under a jurisdiction are chosen by one place, where the organization or site states it is. They are not a determination of which laws apply to you. Privacy law in particular can reach a business through where its customers live.
  • Every report states what the scan did not check. Read that section as part of the report.

You remain solely responsible for your own website and for your legal and regulatory obligations. Decisions you take on the strength of a SITREP are yours.
