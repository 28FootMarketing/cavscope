Why This Matters

Most organizations find out their website has a problem when somebody outside tells them.

A prospect's IT team runs a scan before signing. An accessibility complaint arrives with a screenshot attached. Someone sends invoices from your domain because nothing stopped them. A researcher finds a flaw, has nowhere to report it, and posts it instead.

None of those are discoveries. They are notifications, and by then somebody else set the timeline.

"We didn't know" stops being a position the moment somebody else can prove it was visible.

— the gap MUSTER is built to close
